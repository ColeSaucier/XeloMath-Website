XeloMath

What does the app do?

Designed specifically for young learners of any age, XeloMath is a unique, interactive mobile experience that enhances math learning with engaging gameplay and visualized improvement. Take a hands-off approach to real understanding without boring explanations or instructional videos. With automatic placement, learners immediately start solving problems, push beyond their grade level, and are rewarded for improvement.

Success is measured by speed and progress. Levels are timed so young learners can directly experience their growing fluency. The app requires no reading, making it accessible even for the youngest users. Children learn by interacting with changeable components through guided trial and error. Stars reward progress: one for completion, two for reliable speed, and three for mastery.

One of the most impactful features is the visually present speed component. Instead of a loading bar, the innovative Pace Bar ticks down per rep (or problem) at the rate of an improved personal best. Learners immediately know when they’ve gotten faster. Alongside this, real-time leaderboards display median speed, percentile among peers, and percentage improvement—so parents and kids can see concrete progress (for example, “top 5%” or “+18% faster”). Class cohort leaderboards make it easy for teachers to foster healthy classroom competition.

Why is this app needed?

In traditional education, children primarily learn math in class with a teacher and a textbook. This approach has clear limitations. First, new content is only introduced when the teacher has time to teach it. Second, the teacher cannot easily tailor instruction for fast learners, who are often left repeating redundant material with an averaged approach. Third, teachers rely on textbooks whose written answers take time to check. Fourth, most other math software is not optimized for mobile devices or fails to incorporate improvement-based metrics such as speed, percentiles, and percentage gains.

XeloMath addresses these gaps with a mobile-first, interactive, and improvement-focused design. Automatic placement gets learners started at the right level immediately. Timed challenges, the Pace Bar, percentile rankings, and class cohort leaderboards turn practice into a motivating, measurable experience. Flexibility options let families disable timed features if preferred.

Key Highlights

  • Pace Bar & Speed Metrics: Race against your personal best. See percentage improvement, median speed, and percentile among peers instantly.
  • Class Cohort Leaderboards: Real-time rankings that work great for classrooms. Feel confident when your learner is in the top 5% or better.
  • 30 Progressive Levels: From kindergarten counting through 7th-grade line formulas, exponents, factoring, and more.
  • Automatic Placement: Assesses grade and knowledge so learners start at the right challenge.
  • Playlist & Flexibility: Queue varied levels or disable timed features and leaderboards as needed.
